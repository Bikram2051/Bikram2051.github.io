About me

I am a data analyst with close to four years of experience across market and product insights, reporting and product analytics, and I am currently studying a Master of Data Science at Swinburne University in Melbourne. My background sits between the commercial and the technical. I did an MBA in marketing and business analytics, and then spent my working years in SQL, Python, R and Tableau.

The work I enjoy most is the unglamorous part: getting a messy extract into shape, agreeing what a metric actually means, and then putting it in front of someone in a form they can use. I care about clean assumptions and about being able to rerun something six months later and get the same answer.

How I got here

I started in product operations. Two years at PowerWeave in Mumbai looking after catalogue data and order workflows for a United States wholesale platform taught me to respect a clean spreadsheet, mostly by showing me what a dirty one costs. I then moved to Bangalore and joined KeyGap Software Solutions as a junior customer insights analyst, where the work became analysis full time: SQL pulls, Tableau dashboards, A/B test reporting, and the weekly KPI rollup for the team lead.

Somewhere in that job I noticed I was more interested in the data questions than the campaign ones. Which segment is actually growing. Which signup cohort sticks around. What the dashboard is quietly leaving out. So I moved to Melbourne and started the Master of Data Science to build the technical foundation properly rather than picking it up in fragments.

How I like to work

Ask what decision the number is for, agree the definition before building anything, keep the workings reproducible, and write the thing down so the next person is not starting from scratch. A report that nobody changes their mind because of has not really finished its job.
