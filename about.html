About

Business questions first, then the data.

A data analyst who came up through product and market insights, now building the technical depth to match.

Bikram Bhattarai Data Analyst · MDS at Swinburne · Melbourne

I am a data analyst with nearly four years of offshore experience across market and product insights, reporting, and product analytics, and I am currently completing a Master of Data Science at Swinburne University in Melbourne. My background sits at the intersection of business strategy and data: an MBA in Marketing and Business Analytics on the commercial side, with day to day work in SQL, Python, R, and Tableau on the technical side.

I work primarily in SQL, Python, R, Tableau, and Power BI, with experience in Google Analytics 4 and Excel for marketing side analysis. I care about clean assumptions, reproducible work, and writing the README.

My journey into data

My route into analytics started in product operations. I spent two years at PowerWeave in Mumbai managing catalogue data and order workflows for a US wholesale platform: large datasets, recurring process gaps, and the kind of detail work that teaches you to respect a clean spreadsheet. From there I moved to Bangalore, joined Keygap Software Solutions as a Junior Customer Insights Analyst, and started working on SaaS product and marketing data full time, including SQL pulls, Tableau dashboards, A/B test reporting, NPS analysis, and KPI rollups for the team lead.

That work made one thing obvious: I enjoyed the data side more than the campaign side. The questions that mattered, such as which segment is actually growing, which signup cohort retains, and what a dashboard does not tell us, were all data questions. So I moved to Melbourne, started a Master of Data Science at Swinburne, and I am now building the technical foundation I had been working around. The career change is less a pivot than the part of my last job finally getting its own title.

"I don't want to just produce reports. I want the report to change what the team does on Monday."
