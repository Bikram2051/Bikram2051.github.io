Domain adaptive sentiment analysis

Team project, Swinburne University, completed. My role: data engineer.

A sentiment model that had to work across two very different kinds of writing: casual Reddit comments and formal financial reporting. I owned the data side. That meant pulling in GoEmotions and the Financial PhraseBank, writing the cleaning and standardisation steps so both corpora came out in a consistent shape, and producing the train, validation and test splits the modelling work ran on. The team used those splits for the BERT and FinBERT models and a Streamlit dashboard that displayed the metrics.

Tools  Python, pandas, Hugging Face, BERT and FinBERT

Australian beach and water locations

University project (COS60008), completed.

An analysis of beach and water locations around Australia using open data. Bank loan portfolio dashboard

Personal project.

I loaded a bank loan dataset into MS SQL Server and wrote the queries behind the headline numbers: total applications, funded amount, average interest rate and the month on month movement, using DATEPART, CTEs and window functions. Loans were split into performing and non performing, and the results went into three linked Tableau dashboards covering the summary, the monthly and regional view, and a detail table. Every figure on the dashboards was checked back against the SQL.

Company bankruptcy prediction

Personal project, using the UCI Polish companies dataset.

A classification problem with a heavy imbalance: five yearly snapshots, 64 financial ratios, and very few insolvent companies to learn from. I handled the imbalance with SMOTE and cost sensitive learning, then compared logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting. Recall and ROC AUC mattered more than plain accuracy here, since missing a company that goes under is far more expensive than a false alarm. The feature importances lined up with the liquidity and leverage ratios you would expect.

Tools  Python, scikit-learn, imbalanced-learn
